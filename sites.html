How this works

Only sites I choose is the default. Static watches each page, lists its observation above, and masks a site only after you choose Protect — in the popup or on this page.

Every site masks every page straight away, except the sites you pause. Paused origins are listed here so you can turn them back on. Your per-site choices are kept while this mode is on.

Turning a site on masks that site. Static blocks extension-enumeration probes, rewrites the user agent and Client Hints the page sees, can mask the device signals the page reads when device masking is on, and applies its narrow fingerprint and CAPTCHA vendor lists there. It does not proxy your traffic, add a VPN, block ads or trackers broadly, or send what it observes anywhere: everything stays on this device.

A decision covers a site, not a single page: every subdomain, scheme and port follows it. Autodetection is a hint, not a guarantee — a page can check for extensions before Static’s observer runs, or in ways the observer cannot see. The detection list stays local; decisions and what a page was seen doing never leave the browser.

Forget undecided sites clears the observations for sites without a decision. Clear all decisions clears the protection choices themselves; pages you have chosen stay in the list until you choose again.
